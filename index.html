Your rule

You decide how much
your spending means.

Stakeback isn't an AI telling you what to buy. You set the rule. Your money follows your logic — not ours.

Fixed
₹10 whenever I shop at an eligible business

Simple and predictable. A flat amount invested every time you spend with a connected brand.

Percentage
Invest 2% of eligible spending

Your investment scales with your spending. Bigger purchases mean bigger contributions, proportionally.

Round-up
Round every purchase up and invest the difference

The smallest possible step. The spare change from every transaction gets put to work.

Selective
Only invest when I spend with businesses I choose

Full control. Pick which brands trigger your investing. Skip the rest.

These are conceptual examples of possible future rules, not promises about V1 functionality.
